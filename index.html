      What is Qlip?

      Short games built on models from psychology and practical philosophy: the QUMNI Will·Skill·Opportunity and Perceptual Control frameworks, Kahneman, Senge, Stoics, Aristotle, Dweck, Clear and more.

      Each one takes under a minute, shows you where you stand, names the model, and gives you one thing to try today.

      It's for learning, not a diagnosis or therapy. Results are snapshots of how you answered today. If you're struggling, talk to someone you trust or find a free, confidential helpline at findahelpline.com.

      Your answers stay in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
